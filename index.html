Profile

My research focuses on design engineering based on mathematics, computational mechanics, and informatics, with expertise in numerical methods for multiphysics simulation, method development in topology optimisation, and high-performance computing. I am particularly interested in the optimum design of multi-scale and multiphysics systems — such as heat exchangers and microchannel cooling devices — and their applications in industrial products.

At present, I am extending my research towards network scheduling optimisation, such as district heating systems, coupled with integrated assessment modelling. Alongside research, I lecture on finite element analysis and structural optimisation and supervise Ph.D. and M.Sc. projects.

Topology optimisation Multiphysics simulation High-performance computing Scientific machine learning Heat exchangers District heating
Method development

Multi-scale & multiphysics design

Level-set and density-based topology optimisation, homogenisation-based multi-scale design with dehomogenisation, and body-fitted adaptive meshing for thermal–fluid systems.

Software & HPC

Open, distributed frameworks

Easy-to-use open-source frameworks in FreeFEM and Firedrake, built on PETSc/SLEPc and Mmg/ParMmg for high-resolution 3D design on distributed finite element solvers.

From simulation to prototype

Validation & industrial use

Experimental validation of topology-optimised liquid-cooled channels, and industrial collaborations that turn optimised designs into manufacturable prototypes.

Scientific Skills

Methods, software and experimental practice

Topology optimisation of multi-scale & multiphysics systems

Application and extension of level-set and density-based methods, as well as homogenisation-based multi-scale design with dehomogenisation, to the optimum design of thermal–fluid devices.

Numerical methods for multiphysics simulation

Formulation and solution of PDEs for fluid–structure interaction, conjugate heat transfer, and space–time heat conduction, using distributed finite element solvers with domain decomposition, preconditioners, and adaptive mesh refinement.

Scientific machine learning

Neural-network surrogate models in PyTorch integrated into physics-based design workflows, plus hands-on experience with Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) and Deep Operator Networks (DeepONet) from a research stay at the CRUNCH group, Brown University.

Experimental design & validation

Design and conduct experiments on liquid-cooled channels to validate numerical simulations and bridge the gap between simulation and real-world prototypes.
